# Community Health Clinic Guidance

## Service updates

Clinic hours and appointment rules must come from the official clinic portal. If evening hours
change or walk-in vaccine visits become appointment-only, update client advice only after review.

## Required documents

Insurance or ID requirements are listed on the clinic portal. Do not invent substitute rules.

## Language assistance

Offer language assistance contacts published by the clinic.

## Application support

Help patients read hours and appointment instructions. Never enter protected health information
into Civic Canary.
