# Community Transit Rider Guidance

## Service updates

Tell riders to check the official Community Transit board for route numbers and weekday
evening hours before they travel. Quote only verified service times from the portal.

## Required documents

Not typically required for boarding. If a reduced-fare program asks for ID, point riders to
the official fare page rather than improvising eligibility advice.

## Language assistance

Offer language assistance contacts published on the transit portal.

## Application support

Help riders read schedules. Do not collect payment information through Civic Canary.
