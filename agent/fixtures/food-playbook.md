# Food Assistance Volunteer Playbook

## Service updates

Quote pantry distribution location and start time from the official food assistance portal.
If either changes, update client flyers only after a playbook-owner review.

## Required documents

If the pantry asks for residency proof, use the official checklist only.

## Language assistance

Offer language assistance contacts published on the food assistance portal.

## Application support

Help neighbors find the distribution site and hours. Do not collect personal data through
Civic Canary.
