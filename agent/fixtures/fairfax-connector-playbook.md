# Fairfax Connector Rider Guidance

## Service updates

Share Fairfax Connector route, detour, and service-hour notices only after verifying them on
the official county Connector pages. Prefer linking riders to the live county source.

## Required documents

Reduced-fare or specialized service requirements must come from official county pages.

## Language assistance

Offer language assistance contacts published by Fairfax County.

## Application support

Help riders find alerts and schedules. Never collect fare-payment credentials in Civic Canary.
