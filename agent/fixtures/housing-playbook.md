# Housing Assistance Counselor Playbook

## Service updates

Deadlines and intake windows must be quoted from the housing portal. If a deadline moves,
update client reminders only after a playbook-owner review.

## Required documents

Ask clients to bring only documents listed on the official portal checklist. Do not tell a
client they are eligible based on Civic Canary findings alone.

## Language assistance

Offer language assistance contacts published on the housing portal.

## Application support

Staff may explain field labels. They must never submit an application on a client's behalf
through Civic Canary.
